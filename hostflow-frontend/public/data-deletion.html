B2B context

HostFlow is a B2B SaaS for recruitment agencies and employers. Meta Lead Ads are connected by a company that uses HostFlow as a Tech Provider. Lead data belongs to that business’s hiring process — HostFlow is not a consumer Facebook app for individual users.

How to request data deletion

If you have previously submitted your data through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead form connected to HostFlow Leads and wish to delete it, you can request removal at any time.

To request deletion of your data, please send an email with the subject line “Data Deletion Request — HostFlow Leads” to:

E-mail: info@hostflow.cc

Include in your message the name, phone number, and email address you used in the lead form, so we can locate and delete your record. All confirmed requests are processed within 30 days.

Automatic data retention

Data submitted through lead forms are automatically stored securely in the HostFlow system and are not shared with third parties. They are periodically reviewed and deleted when no longer needed for recruitment or contact purposes.
